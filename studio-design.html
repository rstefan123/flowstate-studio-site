Services

Studio Design

Our in-depth knowledge of all things audio, from both the user’s and designer’s perspective, ensures that your studio will become an extension of yourself, allowing you to enter an effortless creative flow state without ever needing to think or troubleshoot.

Equipment Selection

Whether you are building a new studio from scratch or upgrading an existing studio, we can spec out and source all the necessary audio equipment based on your needs, desires, and budget. We will ensure that all the different components play nicely with each other and fit together in the most efficient and user-friendly way possible. In some cases, we can design and build custom equipment and solutions for more unconventional tasks.

Integration

We will install all your equipment and connect it together for you. From there, we will configure your gear and create templates and layouts to make your daily workflows easier and smoother. By the end of the install, you won’t have to change any settings for everything to work as you expect.

Automation

In order to make your work effortless and intuitive, we can create custom macros for SoundFlow, Keyboard Maestro, BetterTouchTool, or Automator to eliminate tedious, repetitive tasks and allow you to focus on creativity. We can utilize hardware such as Stream Decks, MIDI controllers, lights, smart switches, and much more to make things happen automatically at your command.

Want your record light to automatically turn on when you hit record in Pro Tools? Want to send audio to iZotope RX with a custom gesture on your trackpad? Want every RX module available as a button on your Stream Deck? Want to automatically rename hundreds of clips with one button? Want your Roomba to vacuum the carpet when you hit C# on your keyboard? The possibilities are endless.

Custom Wiring

We will build, run, label, and dress all the necessary cabling to length for a clean, organized, and aesthetically pleasing install. We can solder just about any type of cable for you and design custom patch bay or patch panel layouts. We can even make a custom engraved patch panel or rack spacer with your logo on it.

Client Experience

Your clients and employees can be kept innocent of all these things. To them it will look and feel like everything just works.
